A monthly spending review that doesn’t ask you to log anything.

For Malaysians on Android paying across a few bank accounts, a couple of cards and two or three e-wallets — you keep spending the way you already do. Once a month you get a plain-English read on where it went and what you could realistically have kept.

Android only, and honestly so. The way this would work is by reading the payment notifications your bank and e-wallet apps already send you. Only Android lets an app do that. On an iPhone it is not a missing feature we could add later — it is not possible, so we are not going to promise it to you.
